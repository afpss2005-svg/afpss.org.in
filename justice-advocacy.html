The Organisation's Role

Under the leadership of President Shri Matin Shankarrao Bhosale, the organisation works to bring community concerns together, carry voices to the administration and support eligible families in accessing rights and government programmes.

Its areas of advocacy include documentation, housing, livelihoods, goat rearing, self-help groups, economic development, awareness of government schemes and raising community concerns when injustice or exclusion is reported.

महाराष्ट्रभर संघटित आवाज

A Collective Voice Across Maharashtra

समाजातील एखादा प्रश्न स्थानिक पातळीवर निर्माण झाला तरी त्याकडे व्यापक सामाजिक प्रश्न म्हणून पाहणे आणि योग्य प्रशासनिक स्तरावर तो मांडणे ही संघटनेची कार्यपद्धती आहे. भविष्यातील या पेजवर जिल्हानिहाय उपक्रम, निवेदने, बैठका, आंदोलन/पाठपुरावा आणि त्यातून झालेल्या कार्यवाहीची माहिती दस्तऐवजीकृत स्वरूपात मांडता येईल.

When a community concern arises locally, the organisation's direction is to treat it as part of a wider social question and present it at the appropriate administrative level. This page is designed to document future district-wise initiatives, representations, meetings, advocacy actions and outcomes.
