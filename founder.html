THE BEGINNING

संघर्षातून शिक्षणक्रांतीकडे…

एका व्यक्तीचा नव्हे, तर एका समाजाच्या परिवर्तनासाठी सुरू झालेला प्रवास.

2005

सामाजिक कार्याची दिशा

शिक्षण आणि सामाजिक परिवर्तनाच्या दिशेने मतीन सरांच्या कार्याचा प्रवास आकार घेऊ लागला.

अमरावती जिल्ह्यातील मंगरूळ चव्हाळा या गावातून सुरू झालेला मतीन भोसले यांचा प्रवास हा केवळ एका व्यक्तीच्या संघर्षाची कथा नाही.

हा प्रवास आहे — गरिबीशी लढण्याचा, सामाजिक उपेक्षेला उत्तर देण्याचा, शिक्षणाच्या माध्यमातून परिवर्तन घडवण्याचा आणि फासेपारधी समाजातील मुलांना सन्मानाचे जीवन देण्याचा.

आज हजारो लोकांसाठी प्रेरणास्थान ठरलेले मतीन भोसले सर स्वतः अत्यंत प्रतिकूल परिस्थितीतून पुढे आले. त्यांनी अनुभवलेली उपासमार, भटकंती, सामाजिक भेदभाव आणि शिक्षणाचा अभाव याच गोष्टींना त्यांनी परिवर्तनाची ताकद बनवले.

EARLY LIFE

१. संघर्षमय बालपण

मतीन भोसले यांचे बालपण फासेपारधी समाजातील अत्यंत कठीण परिस्थितीत गेले.

कुटुंबाच्या उदरनिर्वाहासाठी त्यांच्या वडिलांना मजुरीसोबतच जंगलात जाऊन पक्षी आणि लहान प्राण्यांची शिकार करण्याचा प्रयत्न करावा लागत असे. घरात अनेक वेळा अन्नाची कमतरता असे. कुटुंबाला उपासमारीला सामोरे जावे लागले.

फासेपारधी समाजातील अनेक कुटुंबे गावाच्या मुख्य प्रवाहापासून दूर, पाल किंवा तात्पुरत्या निवाऱ्यात राहत होती. शिक्षण, रोजगार, शासकीय कागदपत्रे आणि मूलभूत सुविधा यांचा मोठ्या प्रमाणात अभाव होता.

लहानपणीच मतीन यांनी आपल्या समाजाची ही वेदना जवळून अनुभवली. हीच वेदना पुढे त्यांच्या संपूर्ण जीवनकार्याची प्रेरणा ठरली.

EDUCATION

२. शिक्षणाची पहिली पायरी

मतीन यांच्या बालपणी त्यांच्या वडिलांना एका आरोपाखाली तुरुंगवास भोगावा लागला.

वडील तुरुंगातून बाहेर आल्यानंतर त्यांनी आपल्या मुलांना शाळेत दाखल करण्याचा निर्णय घेतला. मतीन शाळेत गेले.

परंतु शाळेचा प्रवास सोपा नव्हता. फासेपारधी समाजातील मुलगा म्हणून त्यांना भेदभावाचा सामना करावा लागला. काही विद्यार्थ्यांकडून त्यांची चेष्टा केली जात असे.

या वातावरणामुळे काही काळ त्यांनी शाळेत जाणे बंद केले. मात्र त्यांच्या वडिलांनी हार मानली नाही.

काही शिक्षकांनी त्यांना प्रेम आणि आधार दिला. त्यांना गणवेश, दप्तर, पाटी, पुस्तके आणि शैक्षणिक साहित्य मिळाले. मतीन यांनी या मदतीचे उत्तर आपल्या अभ्यासातून दिले.

SOCIAL AWAKENING

३. शिक्षणातून निर्माण झालेली सामाजिक जाणीव

शाळेत ऐकलेल्या कविता, प्रार्थना, गाणी आणि महान व्यक्तिमत्त्वांच्या जीवनकथांनी मतीन यांच्या विचारविश्वाला नवी दिशा दिली.

महात्मा गांधी आणि छत्रपती शिवाजी महाराज यांच्या जीवनातून त्यांना प्रेरणा मिळाली.

त्यांना जाणवले — शिक्षण म्हणजे केवळ नोकरी मिळवण्याचे साधन नाही; शिक्षण म्हणजे समाज बदलण्याची शक्ती आहे.

IDENTITY & RIGHTS

४. जात प्रमाणपत्रासाठी संघर्ष

इयत्ता नववीत असताना मतीन यांना एका रेशन दुकानातील प्रसंगातून आपल्या समाजातील एका मोठ्या समस्येची जाणीव झाली.

त्यांच्या कुटुंबाकडे रेशनकार्ड नव्हते. रेशनकार्ड मिळवण्यासाठी जात प्रमाणपत्र आणि ओळखपत्र आवश्यक होते.

परंतु फासेपारधी समाजातील अनेक कुटुंबांकडे कोणतीही अधिकृत कागदपत्रे नव्हती. मतीन यांनी हा प्रश्न स्वतःपुरता मर्यादित ठेवला नाही.

त्यांनी समाजातील लोकांना एकत्र केले. प्रशासनाकडे पाठपुरावा केला. अर्ज, निवेदने आणि आंदोलनाच्या माध्यमातून समाजाला अधिकृत ओळख मिळवून देण्यासाठी प्रयत्न केले.

हा त्यांच्या सामाजिक कार्यातील पहिला मोठा टप्पा होता.

A NEW DIRECTION

५. CRPF पासून पुन्हा शिक्षणाकडे

दहावीची परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण झाल्यानंतर मतीन यांची CRPF मध्ये निवड झाली. त्यांनी प्रशिक्षणाला सुरुवात केली.

मात्र प्रशिक्षणादरम्यान शारीरिक त्रास आणि दुखापतीमुळे त्यांना प्रशिक्षण सोडावे लागले.

या अपयशाने मतीन खचले नाहीत. त्यांनी पुन्हा शिक्षणाचा मार्ग स्वीकारला आणि 2004 मध्ये बारावीची परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण केली.

TEACHER

६. शिक्षक म्हणून नवी सुरुवात

2005 मध्ये मतीन यांना जिल्हा परिषद शाळेत शिक्षक म्हणून काम करण्याची संधी मिळाली. धारणी परिसरात त्यांनी विद्यार्थ्यांना शिकवले.

शिक्षक म्हणून काम करताना त्यांना एक गोष्ट अधिक स्पष्टपणे जाणवली —

“ज्या शिक्षणामुळे माझे आयुष्य बदलले, त्याच शिक्षणामुळे माझ्या समाजातील मुलांचेही आयुष्य बदलू शकते.”

त्यामुळे नोकरी करत असतानाही ते फासेपारधी समाजातील मुलांच्या प्रश्नांशी जोडलेले राहिले.

TURNING POINT • 2009

७. दोन मुलांनी बदललेले आयुष्य

2009 मधील एका घटनेने मतीन यांच्या आयुष्याला निर्णायक वळण दिले.

फासेपारधी समाजातील दोन लहान मुलांचे मृतदेह एका ओढ्याजवळ आढळल्याची माहिती त्यांना मिळाली. त्या मुलांचे आई-वडील पोलिसांच्या ताब्यात होते.

मतीन घटनास्थळी पोहोचले. त्या दोन निष्पाप मुलांचा मृत्यू पाहून ते अंतर्बाह्य हादरले.

“

मी शिक्षक म्हणून फक्त माझ्या कुटुंबासाठी काम करायचे की माझ्या समाजातील या मुलांसाठीही काही करायचे?

याच घटनेने त्यांच्या आयुष्याची दिशा बदलली. त्यांनी समाजातील वंचित मुलांसाठी आपले आयुष्य समर्पित करण्याचा निर्णय घेतला.

THE SEARCH

८. रस्त्यावरच्या मुलांचा शोध

मतीन यांनी शिक्षकाच्या नोकरीतून विनावेतन रजा घेतली आणि स्वयंसेवकांच्या मदतीने विविध जिल्ह्यांमध्ये फासेपारधी समाजातील मुलांचा शोध सुरू केला.

रेल्वे स्थानके, वाहतूक सिग्नल आणि सार्वजनिक ठिकाणी भीक मागणारी मुले त्यांच्या नजरेस पडत होती. अनेक मुले अनाथ होती. अनेकांच्या आई-वडिलांवर गुन्ह्यांचे आरोप होते किंवा ते तुरुंगात होते.

या मुलांच्या आयुष्यात ना पुरेसे अन्न, ना कपडे, ना सुरक्षित घर आणि ना शिक्षण.

मतीन यांनी मुलांना आश्वासन दिले — “तुम्हाला अन्न मिळेल, निवारा मिळेल आणि शिक्षण मिळेल.”

सुमारे वर्षभराच्या प्रयत्नांनंतर विविध ठिकाणांहून 188 मुलांना एकत्र आणण्यात आले.

THE SCHOOL

९. शिक्षकाची नोकरी सोडून शाळेच्या स्वप्नासाठी

समाजातील मुलांसाठी स्वतंत्र शाळा उभारण्याचा निर्णय मतीन यांनी घेतला. या ध्येयासाठी त्यांनी शिक्षकाची नोकरी सोडली.

2012 मध्ये एका छोट्याशा जागेवर साध्या झोपडीत शाळेची सुरुवात झाली. कुटुंबातील शेळ्या विकून आणि शिक्षक म्हणून केलेल्या बचतीतून शाळेची सुरुवात करण्यात आली.

परंतु शाळा सुरू करणे सोपे होते; शेकडो मुलांचे पोट भरणे हा खरा संघर्ष होता. कधी लोकांकडून भात-डाळ मागून मुलांचे जेवण चालवावे लागले. कधी मुलांना फक्त पातळ भाताचे पेज देण्याची वेळ आली.

हा संघर्ष दीड वर्षापेक्षा अधिक काळ सुरू राहिला.

PEOPLE'S SUPPORT • 2013

१०. भीक नको — मदत मागण्याचा नवा मार्ग

ऑगस्ट 2013 मध्ये मतीन यांनी निधी उभारण्याची पद्धत बदलली.

स्वतः भीक मागण्याऐवजी त्यांनी समाजातील प्रत्येक व्यक्तीला आपल्या शाळेसाठी एक रुपया देण्याचे आवाहन केले.

यानंतर विविध संस्था, सामाजिक कार्यकर्ते, डॉक्टर, वकील, पत्रकार आणि सामान्य नागरिक त्यांच्या कार्याशी जोडले गेले.

डॉ. प्रकाश आमटे यांच्यासह अनेकांनी मदत केली. हळूहळू शाळेला धान्य, कपडे, पुस्तके, स्टेशनरी आणि इतर आवश्यक साहित्य मिळू लागले.

BUILDING THE DREAM • 2014

११. प्रश्नचिन्ह आदिवासी आश्रमशाळेची उभारणी

2014 मध्ये शाळेच्या बांधकामाला सुरुवात झाली. विहिरी खोदण्यात आल्या आणि वर्गखोल्या बांधण्यात आल्या.

ग्रंथालय, स्वच्छतागृहे, खुला मंच, अभ्यासासाठी जागा आणि खेळाचे मैदान तयार करण्यात आले.

दूरदूरच्या भागातून येणाऱ्या विद्यार्थ्यांसाठी वसतिगृहाचीही व्यवस्था करण्यात आली.

“प्रश्नचिन्ह आदिवासी आश्रमशाळा”

१३. रस्त्यावरची मुले मैदानावर आली

ज्या मुलांच्या हातात कधीकाळी भीक मागण्याची वाटी होती, त्या मुलांच्या हातात आता पुस्तके आली.

ज्या मुलांना उद्याची खात्री नव्हती, त्यांच्या डोळ्यांत भविष्याची स्वप्ने दिसू लागली.

अनेक विद्यार्थ्यांनी दहावीची परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण केली. शाळेच्या मुलींच्या कबड्डी संघाने तालुका आणि राज्यस्तरीय स्पर्धांमध्ये यश मिळवले.

ही केवळ क्रीडा क्षेत्रातील कामगिरी नव्हती. हा संदेश होता — वंचित मुलांमध्येही प्रतिभा असते. त्यांना फक्त संधीची गरज असते.

A MAJOR CRISIS

१४. समृद्धी महामार्गामुळे आलेले संकट

ज्या शाळेसाठी मतीन यांनी अनेक वर्षे संघर्ष केला, ती शाळा पुन्हा एका मोठ्या संकटात सापडली.

नागपूर-मुंबई समृद्धी महामार्गाच्या नियोजनामुळे शाळेच्या जागेवर प्रश्नचिन्ह निर्माण झाले. मतीन यांनी प्रशासन, अधिकारी आणि राजकीय नेत्यांकडे वारंवार पाठपुरावा केला.

शाळा वाचवण्यासाठी प्रयत्न केले. परंतु शेवटी बुलडोझर शाळेच्या आवारात आले.

वर्गखोल्या पाडल्या गेल्या. ग्रंथालय उद्ध्वस्त झाले. स्वच्छतागृहे आणि स्वयंपाकघर जमीनदोस्त झाले. खेळाचे मैदान नष्ट झाले.

मतीन यांनी वर्षानुवर्षे लावलेली झाडेही उद्ध्वस्त झाली. एका क्षणात अनेक वर्षांची मेहनत मातीमध्ये मिळाली.

NEVER GIVE UP

१५. पण मतीन भोसले थांबले नाहीत…

शाळेची इमारत पाडली गेली. पण शिक्षणाचे स्वप्न पाडता आले नाही.

शाळेतील शेकडो मुलांचे भविष्य पुन्हा उभे करण्याचे आव्हान मतीन यांच्यासमोर होते.

पाणी, निवारा, वर्गखोल्या, स्वयंपाकघर, ग्रंथालय आणि विद्यार्थ्यांच्या शिक्षणाची व्यवस्था — प्रत्येक प्रश्न पुन्हा नव्याने उभा राहिला.

पण मतीन पुन्हा कामाला लागले. कारण त्यांच्या दृष्टीने ही केवळ इमारत नव्हती.

ही मुलांची घरे होती.
ही मुलांची शाळा होती.
ही त्यांच्या भविष्याची आशा होती.

“

THE BEGINNING

From Struggle to an Educational Revolution

A journey that began with one person's determination to create change for a community.

2005

The Social Mission Takes Shape

The journey towards education, social support and transformation began taking shape.

The journey of Matin Bhosale Sir, which began in Mangrul Chavala, Amravati district, is not merely the story of one individual's struggle.

It is a journey of fighting poverty, responding to social exclusion, creating change through education and working towards a dignified life for children from the Phanse Pardhi community.

Having grown up in extremely difficult circumstances, Matin Sir transformed his experiences of hunger, migration, social discrimination and lack of education into a force for change.

EARLY LIFE

1. A Childhood of Struggle

Matin Bhosale spent his childhood in extremely difficult circumstances within the Phanse Pardhi community.

His father had to depend on labour and efforts to hunt birds and small animals in the forest to support the family. There were frequent shortages of food and periods of hunger.

Many families in the Phanse Pardhi community lived away from the mainstream, often in temporary settlements. Education, employment, official documents and basic facilities were largely unavailable.

Matin experienced this pain of his community from childhood. This experience later became the inspiration for his life's work.

EDUCATION

2. The First Step Towards Education

During Matin's childhood, his father spent time in jail following an allegation. After his release, his father decided to send his children to school.

Matin entered school, but the journey was not easy. As a child from the Phanse Pardhi community, he experienced discrimination and ridicule.

For some time, this environment caused him to stop attending school. His father, however, did not give up.

Some teachers provided him with love and support. He received a uniform, school bag, slate, books and educational materials. Matin responded to this support through his studies.

SOCIAL AWAKENING

3. Social Awareness Through Education

Poems, prayers, songs and the life stories of great personalities he encountered at school gave Matin a new direction.

The lives of Mahatma Gandhi and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj inspired him.

He realised: Education is not merely a means to obtain a job; education is a force capable of transforming society.

IDENTITY & RIGHTS

4. The Struggle for Caste and Identity Documents

While studying in Class 9, Matin became aware of a major problem faced by his community through an incident at a ration shop.

His family did not have a ration card. A caste certificate and identity documents were required to obtain one.

Many Phanse Pardhi families had no official documents. Matin decided not to keep this issue limited to his own family.

He brought members of the community together, followed up with the administration and worked through applications, representations and collective action.

This became one of the first major milestones in his social work.

A NEW DIRECTION

5. From CRPF Training Back to Education

After passing Class 10, Matin was selected for the CRPF and began training.

However, physical difficulties and injury forced him to leave the training.

He did not allow this setback to defeat him. He returned to education and passed his Class 12 examination in 2004.

TEACHER

6. A New Beginning as a Teacher

In 2005, Matin received an opportunity to work as a teacher in a Zilla Parishad school. He taught students in the Dharni area.

As a teacher, one thought became increasingly clear:

“The education that changed my life can also change the future of children from my community.”

Even while working as a teacher, he remained connected with the problems faced by the Phanse Pardhi community.

TURNING POINT • 2009

7. Two Children Changed the Direction of His Life

An incident in 2009 became a decisive turning point in Matin's life.

He received information that the bodies of two young children from the Phanse Pardhi community had been found near a stream. Their parents were in police custody.

Matin reached the location. The deaths of those innocent children deeply shook him.

“

Should I work only for my own family as a teacher, or should I also do something for the children of my community?

This incident changed the direction of his life. He decided to dedicate his life to deprived children.

THE SEARCH

8. Searching for Children on the Streets

Matin took leave without pay from his teaching job and began searching for children from the Phanse Pardhi community with the help of volunteers.

He found children begging at railway stations, traffic signals and public places. Many were orphans. In many cases, their parents faced criminal allegations or were in jail.

These children had insufficient food, clothing, shelter and access to education.

Matin promised them: “You will have food, shelter and education.”

After nearly a year of efforts, 188 children were brought together from different locations.

THE SCHOOL

9. Leaving Teaching to Build a School

Matin decided to establish a school specifically for children from his community. He left his teaching job for this mission.

In 2012, the school began in a simple hut on a small piece of land. The beginning was supported by selling family goats and using savings earned while working as a teacher.

Starting a school was one challenge; feeding hundreds of children was another. At times, rice and dal had to be collected from people to provide meals. Sometimes the children had only thin rice porridge.

This struggle continued for more than a year and a half.

PEOPLE'S SUPPORT • 2013

10. A New Way of Asking for Support

In August 2013, Matin changed the way he sought financial support.

Instead of begging himself, he appealed to people in society to contribute one rupee for the school.

Institutions, social workers, doctors, lawyers, journalists and ordinary citizens gradually joined the mission.

Dr. Prakash Amte and many others also extended support. The school began receiving food grains, clothes, books, stationery and other materials.

BUILDING THE DREAM • 2014

11. Building Prashnachinh Adivasi Ashram School

Construction of the school began in 2014. Wells were developed and classrooms were built.

A library, sanitation facilities, an open stage, study areas and a playground were developed.

Residential arrangements were also created for students coming from distant areas.

“Prashnachinh Adivasi Ashram School”

The name itself carried a deep question.

What is the future of the Phanse Pardhi community?
What is the future of these children?
Will the school receive recognition?

The name emerged from these questions — the many question marks surrounding their future.

GROWTH

12. The School Grew — The Dream Grew

188 Initial students
450+ Students today

STUDENTS • SPORTS • DREAMS

13. Children from the Streets Reached the Playing Field

Children who once held begging bowls began holding books.

Children who had no certainty about tomorrow began to see dreams for their future.

Many students passed Class 10. The girls' kabaddi team achieved success at taluka and state-level competitions.

This was not merely sporting achievement. It carried a larger message: Deprived children also have talent. They simply need an opportunity.

A MAJOR CRISIS

14. The Crisis Caused by the Samruddhi Expressway

The school that Matin had worked for over many years faced another major crisis.

Planning for the Nagpur-Mumbai Samruddhi Expressway created a question over the school's land. Matin repeatedly followed up with administrators, officials and political leaders.

He tried to save the school. Eventually, bulldozers entered the school premises.

Classrooms were demolished. The library was destroyed. Toilets and the kitchen were demolished. The playground was lost.

Even trees planted by Matin over many years were destroyed. Years of work were reduced to rubble.

NEVER GIVE UP

15. But Matin Bhosale Did Not Stop…

The school building was demolished. But the dream of education could not be demolished.

The challenge before Matin was to rebuild the future of hundreds of students.

Water, shelter, classrooms, kitchen, library and education arrangements — every question appeared again.

But Matin started working again. Because for him, this was not merely a building.

It was the children's home.
It was the children's school.
It was their hope for the future.

THE HEART OF THE MISSION

16. “I Was, Am and Will Remain a Mother to These Children…”

“

I was, am and will remain a mother to these children.

A reflection of the humanity behind his mission.

These children were not merely students to him. They were his responsibility.

He continued fighting so that children brought from footpaths, railway stations and traffic signals would never have to return to the same circumstances.

PEOPLE BEHIND THE JOURNEY

17. A Struggle Sustained by Society

Matin was not alone in his struggle.

Journalists, social workers, teachers, doctors, lawyers, institutions, donors and ordinary citizens came forward.

Some gave food grains.
Some gave clothes.
Some gave books.
Some provided financial support.
Some gave their time.

This collective support helped Prashnachinh Ashram School continue its work.

A THOUGHT • A MOVEMENT

18. Matin Bhosale — More Than One Person, A Thought

Matin Bhosale's story is not merely a story of one person's success.

It represents the beginning of transformation for an entire community.

Children who were marginalised, children who held begging bowls instead of books, children who lived under temporary shelters and children who were kept away from education — Matin has worked to bring them into the mainstream of education.

THE BELIEF

19. His Belief

“

Real change begins through education.

— Matin Shankarrao Bhosale Sir

Matin experienced the transformation education brought to his own life. That is why he considers education the strongest tool for his community's children.

For him, a school is not merely four walls.

A school is a safe home.
A school is food.
A school is values.
A school is confidence.
A school is the future.

THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

20. The Journey Continues Today

Matin Bhosale's journey is not over. His goal remains the same —

to bring deprived, orphaned and extremely vulnerable children from the Phanse Pardhi community into the mainstream of education.

To ensure they receive nutritious food.
To provide safe shelter.
To place books in their hands.
To give them opportunities in sports and skills.
To help them live with dignity in society.

And most importantly — to ensure that parents do not have to look at their children and ask, “What will happen to their future?”

VISION

21. Matin Sir's Mission

“

To provide education, a safe environment, love and a better future to children growing up on streets, footpaths and in extremely difficult circumstances.

A STORY THE WORLD SHOULD KNOW

22. An Inspirational Story the World Should Know

A boy from a poor Phanse Pardhi family…

A student who faced discrimination…

A young man who struggled for identity documents…

A young man who left CRPF training…

A teacher…

A social worker searching for children on the streets…

A worker who brought together 188 children…

A teacher who began a school in a hut…

A founder who built an Ashram School for hundreds of children…

And a determined person who, even after watching the school being demolished, stood up again and continued the struggle…

When we give one child an opportunity to learn, we do not change only one life; we change the future of a family, a community and the next generation.
