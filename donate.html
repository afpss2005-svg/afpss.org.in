Frequently Asked Questions

Donation FAQs

❓ Is my donation secure?

Yes. Donations are made directly to the official AFPSS bank account or UPI.

❓ Will I receive a donation receipt?

Yes. An official receipt can be provided for every eligible donation.

❓ Is the donation eligible for tax benefits?

Yes. Eligible donations may qualify under the 80G provisions, subject to applicable rules.

❓ How is my donation used?

Your support helps provide education, food, hostel facilities, healthcare and learning resources for tribal children.
